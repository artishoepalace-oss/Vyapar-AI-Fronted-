# Vyapar AI 20.10.2004.00001.2026

Low-border surface hierarchy release.

## Visual changes
- Outer card/container borders are removed or reduced to near-invisible separators.
- Card surfaces are only slightly lighter than the page background.
- Heavy shadows are removed; only minimal structural depth remains on header, dialogs, and navigation.
- Nested cards are visually flattened into borderless inner surfaces.
- Card and tool padding is tighter for a denser business-app layout.
- Sections are separated primarily with spacing and tonal contrast instead of outlines.
- Bottom navigation outer border and shadow are substantially subtler.
- Same surface hierarchy layer is mirrored to Android and Web.
