# Vyapar AI 8.5.7.2026

## iOS-style convex liquid glass polish

- Reworked the red/green chromatic accents into behaving convex lens edges instead of flat decorative borders.
- Lens response follows touch/pointer position and shifts the green/red refraction subtly across supported glass surfaces.
- Replaced milky/Aero-like glass with smoky grey water-glass surfaces for the header, navigation, login, popups, Shop Journey and Settings.
- Unified More and Shop Journey popup material, corner geometry, close controls and motion language.
- Polished the login form into one continuous rounded iOS-style surface while preserving existing auth logic.
- Added Android 7/8 performance fallbacks for expensive live blur/refraction effects.

## Smarter recommendation

- Home/Shop Progress now presents a contextual “Next Best Action” card.
- Recommendation changes from real saved state, including first sale, low stock, customer dues, or end-of-day business review.
- Recommendation actions open the relevant Sales, Stock or Business workspace directly.
